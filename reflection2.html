Reflection 2: Photography & Photoshop Basics

Ever since I enrolled in this class, I was very excited in regard to doing photography as part of the syllabus. Learning photography in the more complex aspect has given me a new experience on the process of the assignment. During the laboratory class, we were assigned by taking Canon DSLR cameras, taking five pictures of various spaces within the north building of Hunter College. I learned the many different modes and features of the camera, due to being my first using these specific cameras. After I took the pictures, I had to upload them to my laptop as JEPGs, editing two of the five images on Adobe Photoshop. Before working on photoshop, I took notes in understanding the basics of Phundersaotoshop on LinkedIn Learning. The required readings helped me become more confident, because they demonstrated different techniques of image editing such as cropping, level, curves, learning bit depth, color mode, and image sizing. In having no prior experience with using Photoshop or any Adobe software, I did feel overwhelmed from when I first tried it by myself before going through the readings, but I overcame it with the readings. They provided resourceful information in using Photoshop. From what I learned, I learned about the different effects, color saturation, adjusting and contrasting dark and light in layers. Photoshop helped develop a skill that is useful for graphic design or any skill in the field of Media. Photography and Photoshop work in sync animating images. I’m elated to learn and improve my software skills.
